Agent Memory Architecture

How Hindsight empowers DayStart India

1 1. Observe & RetrieveUpon booking request, the AI Agent queries Hindsight with scoped service and site tags.
2 2. Reason & SynthesizeThe Agent detects recurring patterns (e.g. gate locks, heat timing) and formulates an actionable plan with citations.
3 3. Coordinator ApprovalThe coordinator reviews recommendations with 1-click Accept, Edit, or Ignore.
4 4. Deterministic GuardrailsPlatform rules strictly govern worker eligibility (verified, online, rating ≥ 3.0, 10 km). Memory cannot override safety rules.
5 5. Learn from CompletionUpon job completion, safe non-sensitive outcomes are retained into Hindsight for the next booking.
